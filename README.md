Create a professional hackathon README.md for my project "CivicConnect AI".

CivicConnect AI is an AI-powered civic issue reporting and intelligent department routing platform.

Problem:
Citizens often see potholes, broken streetlights, overflowing garbage bins, water leakage, traffic signal problems and other civic issues but may not know which government department is responsible for resolving them.

Solution:
CivicConnect AI allows citizens to describe or photograph a civic issue, provide its location, and use AI-assisted classification to identify the issue type, priority and responsible department. The platform generates a formal complaint and allows citizens to track the complaint. An issue map and authority dashboard provide visualization and analytics.
